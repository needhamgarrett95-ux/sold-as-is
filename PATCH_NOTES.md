# Sold As-Is PC — Patch Notes

## October 16, 2026
- Fixed: the window's top bar could cover the browser buttons and site header. Everything has its own row now.
- The Road Trip travel system was rebuilt from the ground up. Your deals still drop green pins, you still plan up to 3 stops with full fuel math — but now the whole trip actually runs: the truck drives leg by leg, bikes load at each stop, and they land in your garage when you're home. Gas stations work mid-trip, and if you run dry you get towed home with the bikes.
- New: you can type with your physical keyboard on PC — no more on-screen keyboard for value fields.
- New: a power button in the taskbar to close the game.
- You can start a trip as long as you've got enough fuel for the first leg — the game warns you to hit gas stations along the way.
- Listings, your deals, and gas stations all show on the map at all times now — the pin toggles are gone.
- The "Search by moped" dropdown now floats above the listings instead of hiding behind them.

## October 15, 2026
- New: Road Trip travel system. Strike a deal and its pin turns green — pick up to 3 and plan your route with real fuel math. See every active bike listing pinned on a map of the Olympic Peninsula, with distances and drive times from your garage.
- Fixed: the game could flash the marketplace for a split second on launch. It now opens straight to the desktop.
- Desktop icons are easier to read in light mode, and every icon label sits on a frosted pill so it reads on any wallpaper.
- The launcher now finds your game install wherever it landed, so updates finish properly.
- If an update is available, the Play button waits until you're on the latest build.
- The launcher and the game installer now use the real Sold As-Is icon.
- The game opens straight into fullscreen. Press F11 to switch in and out.
- Fixed: the phone "swipe up" screen could flash when the game started. It's gone.
- You can now change your desktop wallpaper: right-click the desktop, hit Personalize. Five built-ins, or upload your own picture.
- The logo and location in the top-right are now a proper widget.

## October 14, 2026
- First PC test build: the full game in a desktop window, with a taskbar and app icons.
